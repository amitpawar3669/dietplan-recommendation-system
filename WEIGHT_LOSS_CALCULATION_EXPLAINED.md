# 📊 Correct Calculation: 5 kg in 3 Weeks

## Your Goal

## Step-by-Step Calculation

### Step 1: Calculate Required Weekly Loss
```
Total weight to lose: 5 kg
Duration: 3 weeks

Weekly loss needed = 5 kg ÷ 3 weeks = 1.67 kg per week
```

### Step 2: Calculate Required Daily Calorie Deficit
**Formula:** (kg per week × 7700 kcal) ÷ 7 days

```
Daily deficit needed = (1.67 × 7700) ÷ 7
Daily deficit needed = 12,859 ÷ 7
Daily deficit needed = 1,837 kcal per day
```

### Step 3: Compare to Safe Limits

| Metric | Your Goal | Safe Range | Status |
|--------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Weekly loss | 1.67 kg/week | 0.5-1.0 kg/week | ❌ TOO FAST |

## 🧮 Mathematical Proof

### Why 0.64 kg/week is Correct for 700 kcal Deficit:

**The formula:**
```
Weight loss (kg) = Calorie deficit (kcal) / 7700
```

**Proof:**
- 1 kg of body fat = 7700 kcal
- Daily deficit of 700 kcal = 700 × 7 days = 4,900 kcal per week
- 4,900 kcal ÷ 7700 kcal/kg = 0.636 kg per week ✓

**This is NOT wrong - it's correct!**
